  2. Korea

      3. Quick Overview of Seoul, South Korea
      Seoul is a hyper-modern metropolis deeply rooted in centuries of history.
      As the capital of South Korea, it seamlessly blends futuristic skyscrapers, K-pop culture, and high-tech
      public transit with ancient palaces, Buddhist temples, and bustling traditional markets. Surrounded by mountainous terrain and split by the Han River,
      Seoul is a vibrant city that truly never sleeps, famous for its unmatched food scene, cafe culture, and safety.

  3. Japan

      4. Quick Overview of Japan
      Japan is an island nation where ultra-futuristic innovation coexists peacefully with deep-rooted traditions.
      Known for its incredible safety, world-class public transportation system (including the famous bullet trains), and meticulous hospitality (omotenashi),
      Japan offers a fascinating cultural contrast. From snow-capped mountains and serene bamboo forests to neon-lit mega-cities and tranquil Zen temples,
      it is a country that captivates every traveler.

  4. New Zeland

      5. Quick Overview of New Zeland New Zealand—known in the indigenous Māori language as Aotearoa, meaning "Land of the Long White Cloud"
      is an island nation situated in the southwestern Pacific Ocean. Located roughly 1,600 kilometers (1,000 miles) southeast of Australia,
      the country is famously isolated, giving its ecosystems and society a distinct character.


  5. Thailand

      6. Quick Overview of Thiland also known as the "Land of Smiles,"
      Thailand is a vibrant tropical destination famous for its ornate Buddhist temples, chaotic and energetic capital,
      rich cultural heritage, and world-renowned street food.
      Beyond the cities lie some of the world’s most stunning tropical islands, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and lush mountainous jungles in the north.
